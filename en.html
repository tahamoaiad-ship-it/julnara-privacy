Privacy Policy — Julnara

Last updated: 24 October 2026 · release 9 · Free · 18+ · package app.julnara.mobile

1. Who we are

Julnara is an Android voice assistant. Wake phrase detection for «جلنارا» runs on-device. After a wake, command audio and text may be sent to the developer’s server and processed by OpenAI and ElevenLabs to transcribe, understand, and speak replies (product voice «Ghada» when enabled). The app is for users 18+.

2. What we collect and how it is processed

  • On-device wake audio: about 20 enrollment samples of «جلنارا» stay on the device for speaker/wake matching. Not uploaded as a voice archive.
  • Command audio after wake: sent over HTTPS to the developer server (/v1/hear) and to OpenAI speech-to-text. Processed ephemerally; we do not keep a permanent audio archive of your commands.
  • Command / reply text: sent to the server (/v1/understand) and may be passed to OpenAI Chat for an AI reply when the cloud path is active.
  • Spoken replies: reply text is converted to speech via ElevenLabs or OpenAI Speech on the server (/v1/speak) and returned to the device for playback.
  • Install / device token: created to bind the install to the server for spend and abuse limits — not for advertising.
  • Local data: tasks, reminders, notes, settings on device.
  • Connect chat: messages you type in Connect may be sent to our server and then to OpenAI to generate a reply. We do not store chat text on our server; the transcript stays on your phone.
  • Support tickets: when you ask for help, a short problem summary may be sent only after your explicit consent in a dialog — not your full chat or voice recordings.
  • Crash reports: with your consent only, technical data (e.g. stack trace, app version) may be sent to help us fix faults, avoiding full conversation audio where possible.
  • Optional training: “Help Julnara learn” is off by default with a separate consent toggle. If enabled, short teaching examples may be stored to improve understanding; you can turn it off and delete data via Delete my data.
  • Interpreter: in release 9, speech is transcribed on the server (/v1/hear). Android SpeechRecognizer is not enabled as the primary path.
  • No voice call: the direct voice-call feature with Julnara in Connect was removed in release 9. Text chat and in-thread voice messages remain.
  • In-app report: may send a description of an inappropriate AI reply to the server for review.

3. Processors

The developer server calls OpenAI (transcription and/or chat and/or speech) and ElevenLabs (Ghada TTS when enabled). API keys stay on the server, not in the APK. Transit is HTTPS.

4. Retention

  • Audio: we do not keep a permanent command-audio archive; processor OpenAI may retain API request data under its policy for up to about 30 days.
  • Text/logs: may be kept briefly for operations and spend limits, with conversation content minimized where the design allows; OpenAI may likewise retain request data for up to about 30 days.
  • Device token and spend counters on the server while you use the service.

5. Deletion

In-app «احذف بياناتي» / Delete my data (confirm dialog; fails closed without a valid subject where required). Email us below. Uninstalling clears local app data.

6. Permissions

  • Microphone + microphone foreground service: wake and commands.
  • Internet: server and processors.
  • Notifications, exact alarms, set alarm, boot completed, optional overlay.

7. Children and ads

18+ only. No ads. We do not sell your data.
