Privacy Policy — Julnara

Last updated: 22 October 2026 · Free · 18+

1. Who is Julnara?

Julnara is a personal Android voice helper (package app.julnara.mobile). She wakes on «جلنارا», answers in the product voice («Ghada» when enabled via the server), and runs commands such as tasks, reminders, and flashlight. Playful Syrian persona and open-ended AI chat mean the app is for users 18+.

2. Privacy principles

  • On-device first: wake detection, owner enrollment, tasks, reminders, and optional memory stay on the phone where possible.
  • We do not sell your data or use chats for advertising.
  • No permanent cloud voice archive of your voice.
  • Provider API keys stay on the developer’s server, not in the app binary.

3. Data on your device

  • Wake-phrase samples of «جلنارا» (first-run ~20 samples) for on-device speaker enrollment.
  • Tasks, reminders, notes, and optional memory if you use them.
  • Language settings, consents, listening state.
  • Per-install device id when linked to the server (rate limits — not ads).

4. Server and cloud processing (when enabled)

When the server path is enabled, the app may send to the developer-operated Julnara server (currently the developer’s PC via a public HTTPS tunnel; later possibly cloud hosting under the same rules):

  • A short command audio clip to speech-to-text (/v1/hear) for immediate processing — not for building a lasting voice database.
  • Request/reply text for understanding and answers; providers (e.g. OpenAI for transcription/understanding, ElevenLabs or another voice path for spoken replies) are used only to serve you, under the developer’s spend caps.
  • A registered device token plus per-device and monthly rate/spend limits on the server.
  • Operational logs are designed to minimize or strip full conversation text where the product requires it.

Offline or when the server is unreachable, local commands (e.g. time, flashlight, tasks) still work within the build’s capabilities.

5. Optional Connect screen

In-app text/voice chat with Julnara. Contributions or uploads to the developer happen only after explicit consent and a send you choose. Withdraw where settings allow.

6. Permissions (aligned with the 1.0 manifest)

  • Microphone + microphone foreground service: wake and commands while listening is enabled.
  • Notifications: reminders and alerts.
  • Internet: server path.
  • Flashlight / optional non-required camera features: torch only; full CAMERA permission is not required for 1.0.
  • Exact alarm scheduling + system SET_ALARM: reminders and handing off to the Clock app when needed.
  • Boot completed: resume reminders/service as designed.
  • System alert window (optional): floating Julnara face if you opt in.

Version 1.0 does not rely on camera, contacts, precise location, Bluetooth, or reading other apps’ notifications as core product features.

7. Children and billing

Not directed at users under 18. No mandatory in-app subscription in 1.0; operator cost caps are on the developer, not billed to you under this policy.

8. Your rights

  • Delete owner data where in-app screens exist.
  • Withdraw consents and stop listening.
  • Uninstall to clear local data (removes wake enrollment and local tasks/reminders; no full automatic export in 1.0).
