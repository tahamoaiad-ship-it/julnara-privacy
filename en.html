Privacy Policy — Julnara Pop

Last updated: 22 October 2026 · Free app · Age rating: 18+

1. Who is Julnara?

Julnara Pop («جلنارا بوب») is a personal Android voice helper. She wakes on the phrase «جلنارا», answers in her product voice («Ghada» when enabled), and runs commands such as tasks, reminders, and flashlight. She has a warm Syrian persona; open-ended AI chat means the app is for users 18+.

2. Privacy principles

  • On-device first: wake detection, owner-voice enrollment, tasks, reminders, and optional memory stay on your phone where possible.
  • No sale of your data. No ads built from your chats.
  • No permanent cloud voice archive of your voice. Owner audio is not uploaded or stored without explicit consent.
  • API secrets stay on the server, not inside the app binary.

3. What stays on your phone

  • Wake-phrase samples of «جلنارا» for speaker enrollment (first-run: about 20 samples).
  • Tasks, reminders, notes, and optional memory if you use those features.
  • Language settings, consents, and listening state.
  • A per-install device id when linked to the server (for rate limits — not advertising).

4. When the server is used

For speech understanding and general chat, text of your request (after speech-to-text) may go to the Julnara server run by the developer — currently on the developer’s PC via a secure HTTPS tunnel, and later possibly on cloud hosting under the same privacy rules.

  • The server may call processing providers (language understanding or spoken replies) only to serve you, under operator spend caps — not to sell your data as a product.
  • We do not keep raw voice recordings as a lasting cloud voice database.
  • Operational logs are designed to avoid full conversation text where the product requires it.

5. Optional Connect screen

In-app text/voice chat with Julnara. Sending contributions to the developer happens only after explicit consent and a send action you choose. You can withdraw where settings allow.

6. Permissions

  • Microphone: wake, commands, and the foreground listening service when enabled.
  • Notifications: reminders and alerts.
  • Internet: when using the server path.
  • Other permissions (e.g. optional overlay) only if you opt into that feature.

Version 1.0 does not require camera, contacts, or precise location as core product permissions.

7. Children and billing

Not directed at users under 18. Version 1.0 has no mandatory in-app subscription; operator cost caps are on the developer side and are not billed to you under this policy.

8. Your rights

  • Delete owner data in-app where deletion screens exist.
  • Withdraw consents and stop listening.
  • Uninstall to remove local data on the device.

Note: uninstall clears wake enrollment and local tasks/reminders unless you copied them manually first (no full automatic export in 1.0).
