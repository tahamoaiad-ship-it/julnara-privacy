Privacy Policy — Julnara Pop

Last updated: 22 October 2026 · Age rating: 18+

Who we are

Julnara Pop («جلنارا بوب») is a personal on-phone voice helper. This policy explains what is collected and how it is used.

What we collect

  • On device: wake-phrase samples of «جلنارا» for speaker enrollment; tasks, reminders, notes, and optional memory. Stored locally where possible.
  • When server/chat is enabled: command or reply text may be sent to a processing server for speech understanding and answers. We do not keep raw voice recordings as a permanent cloud voice archive.
  • Optional Connect contribution: text/audio you choose to send only after explicit consent.

What we do not do

  • We do not store your voice as a permanent cloud voice database.
  • We do not use your chats to train public AI models unless you explicitly opt in to contribution/training (optional; off by default).
  • We do not sell your data.
  • Version 1.0 does not require in-app purchase accounts; any operator cost caps are on the developer side and are not billed to you as a mandatory subscription under this policy.

Permissions

Microphone for wake and commands. Notifications for reminders. Other permissions only when you opt into a specific in-scope feature.

Age

The app is intended for users 18 years and older (playful conversational persona and open-ended AI chat).

Your rights

You can delete owner data where in-app deletion screens exist, withdraw consents, and uninstall the app to remove local data.

Contact

For privacy questions about Julnara Pop, use the support channel listed by the developer on the store listing or inside the app.
